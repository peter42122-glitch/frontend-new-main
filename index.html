About Zain Tailor House

Crafting Timeless Style Since 2008

Located in the heart of Abbottabad, Zain Tailor House has built an unrivaled reputation for precision tailoring, custom fits, and timeless elegance. Founded on the principles of traditional craftsmanship combined with modern fashion aesthetics, our master tailors treat every garment as a piece of art.

We focus on accurate physical measurements, premium inner structuring, hand-finished collars, and flawless stitch density to ensure every outfit feels comfortable and looks extraordinary.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide high-quality tailoring with perfect fitting, excellent craftsmanship, and exceptional customer service.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted destination for premium traditional and modern tailoring.

Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? Find clear answers regarding our fitting process, timelines, and services.

Do you provide custom measurements?

Yes, customers can receive professional in-person measurements at our Abbottabad studio for customized stitching, or send a sample garment that fits them perfectly.

How long does stitching take?

Standard stitching takes approximately 3 to 7 working days depending on the garment complexity. Express stitching for urgent events is also available upon request.

Do you provide alterations?

Yes, professional alteration services are available for suits, Shalwar Kameez, pants, shirts, and ladies dresses to improve fitting and comfort.

Can I book an appointment online?

Yes, customers can easily submit an appointment request through our online booking form on this website or reach out via WhatsApp.

Do you stitch wedding dresses?

Yes, we specialize in premium royal Sherwanis, waistcoats, 3-piece wedding suits, and bridal party outfits with custom embroidery and luxury fabrics.
